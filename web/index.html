2 SECOND ARM LIMIT

Prepare once with FC USB connected. Then unplug USB if you want, keep throttle fully down and SA low, and click Start. After the status says ready, move SA to your saved high position. Motors may spin at idle. Lowering SA or pressing Stop disarms; ARM also goes low automatically after two seconds.
